# SFANDOM Instagram Reels Daily Workflow

Last updated: 2026-09-17

## Purpose
Add one Instagram Reel to the daily SFANDOM Work routine.

## Fixed rules
- Produce one Reel every day based on the previous day's sports content, match, player, or notable moment.
- Sport is not fixed. Choose the best subject regardless of sport.
- Target duration: about 10 seconds.
- Final deliverable: MP4.
- Do not use Runway.
- Visual direction should feel native to Instagram: fast, clean, emotional, and immediately readable on mobile.
- Change the theme and mood every day. Avoid repetitive formats.

## Creative rotation examples
- Emotional highlight
- High-tension match moment
- Comeback / decisive play
- One-line data insight
- Single-player spotlight
- Short sports storytelling
- Crowd / atmosphere focus
- Unexpected or funny moment
- Before-and-after momentum shift

## Daily execution flow
1. Review the previous day's matches and sports stories.
2. Select one subject with strong short-form visual potential.
3. Choose a style that was not recently repeated.
4. Edit to roughly 10 seconds with a strong first 1-2 seconds.
5. Keep text minimal and mobile-readable.
6. Export as MP4.
7. Treat the Reel as part of the standard daily Work checklist.

## Trigger phrase
When JI says “오늘 릴스 가자”, proceed using these rules and choose the topic automatically unless a specific topic is given.
